# Example: Tiny Lantern (placeholder project)

Example brief demonstrating the template; replace it with your own idea.

## Snapshot
- Working title: Tiny Lantern
- Genre: Single-player exploration puzzle prototype
- Pitch: Guide a small lantern creature through a quiet forest by redirecting light to reveal safe paths.
- Target players: People who enjoy short, low-pressure environmental puzzles
- Platform / engine: TBD
- Team/time: Solo prototype, two weekends (assumption)

## Player experience
- Fantasy: Be a curious light in a welcoming dark world.
- Pillars: readable light puzzles; gentle exploration; expressive movement.
- Core loop: explore → notice a light clue → reposition light → reveal a route → discover a new space.
- Session: 10–15 minutes for the prototype (assumption)

## Scope
- MVP: one compact level, movement and interaction, two light interactions, one checkpoint.
- Out of scope: combat, online features, procedural generation, monetization.

## Risks and validation
- Risk: light/shadow readability across displays. Test in grayscale and low brightness.
- Accessibility: adjustable brightness and a non-color-only interaction cue.
- Use original/licensed placeholders. Test a paper or greybox prototype before final art.
- Success signal: first-time players can explain the light interaction without coaching.
