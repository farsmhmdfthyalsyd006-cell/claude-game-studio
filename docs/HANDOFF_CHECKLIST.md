# Studio hand-off checklist

## Design → Engineering
- Player goal and flow are clear.
- Rules, states, edge cases, and acceptance criteria are documented.
- Dependencies and tuning knobs are identified.

## Engineering → QA
- Build/commit and setup steps are recorded.
- Changed systems and known limitations are listed.
- Expected behavior and available logs are clear.

## Art/Audio → Integration
- Purpose, format, naming, import settings, and asset provenance are documented.
- Variants, states, accessibility, and fallback behavior are clear.

## QA/Playtest → Team
- Evidence is separated from interpretation.
- Bugs are reproducible and prioritized by player impact.
- Next actions have an owner/role and a verification method.
