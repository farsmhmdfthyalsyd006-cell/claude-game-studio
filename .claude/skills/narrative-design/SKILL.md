# Narrative Design
1. Review pillars, player agency, audience, tone, and content constraints.
2. Define dramatic question, premise, themes, and what the player can change.
3. Choose a suitable structure: beats, quests, scenes, branches, or environmental clues.
4. Specify characters by goal, conflict, voice, relationship, and gameplay function.
5. Draft dialogue with context, intent, choice consequences, and localization-friendly lines.
6. Identify implementation needs: triggers, flags, barks, cutscenes, subtitles, VO, and fallback behavior.
7. Check continuity, choice clarity, accessibility, and feasible content volume.

Separate canon, proposal, and unresolved questions. Use original material; do not imitate a living author's distinctive style.
